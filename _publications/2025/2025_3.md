abstract: >-
  This study unveils the critical role of <strong>dissipative Faraday instability (DFI)</strong> in facilitating the self-starting process of Mamyshev oscillators (MOs). We propose a novel <strong>timing-injection locking</strong> technique to customize pulse temporal locations, offering significant potential for all-optical data storage and tunable ultrashort pulse sources.
